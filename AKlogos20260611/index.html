  • Clear space: keep at least the frame-to-monogram band (about 5% of logo width) free on all sides.
  • Minimum sizes: stacked logo 64px tall, lockup 24px tall; below that use the favicon marks.
  • Do not recolor outside the palette, stretch, rotate, or set the wordmark in another font.
  • Geometry: every gap and stroke follows one golden ratio chain (11.7 : 19 : 30.7 : 49.7).
